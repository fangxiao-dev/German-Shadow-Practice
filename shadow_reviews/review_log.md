This is the durable Markdown log for final report outcomes; keep it separate from YAML state.

Convention for copying final report outcomes here:
- The report step first drafts proposed updates in review_drafts.
- After the user confirms the batch, copy the final outcome into this log as a short entry.
- Record the review date, scope, summary, and the confirmed batch changes.
- Keep the wording compact and factual; do not duplicate the full draft.

Suggested entry shape:
- Date and scope on the first line.
- 1 short summary paragraph.
- 1 short bullet list of confirmed updates, if any.
